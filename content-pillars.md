# ArangoRAW Content Pillars

Built with Alejandro Arango on September 25, 2026. Replaces the six app pillars (Psychology of Chaos, Media Intelligence, Identity Shift, Physics of Business, The Survivor, External Mirrors) and the four pillars in the 2026 Reels Strategy.

## Positioning

**Who it's for.** People who never felt the system was built for them. They've been trying and struggling, they want to reach a different level, and they want creative, unusual ways to get there. Founders of their own life, whether or not they run a company, broke or billionaire.

**Who Alejo is on screen.** The acting force. He lived it, lost it, rebuilt it four times, and he's building again right now. The philosophy lives in the message. He doesn't play the philosopher.

**What content has to bring in.** Speaking gigs, book readers, community members. Client work is out of scope.

**One-line options** (to replace "philosophical media for founders"):
- ArangoRAW. Be the founder of your own life.
- ArangoRAW. A force of nature, on camera, building a life the system never planned for.
- ArangoRAW. Real stories. Wild comebacks. Your turn.

---

## Pillar 1: The Wonder Years

**Description.** True stories from Alejo's life, told fast and visual like movie scenes. Medellín in the violent years, Canada as a teenager, the army, film, Contento, the takeover, Mois, the hospital, leaving Colombia. Every piece is one scene with one turn, and it ends on what it made him. Sources are the book, *After the Wonder Years*, and the story library. Nothing invented.

**Audience.** Strangers scrolling. They don't know Alejo and aren't looking for help. They stay because the story is too wild to skip. Cold, top of funnel.

**Business outcome.** Audience growth and book readers. These stories are also the raw material for keynotes.

**Example topics.**
- One day old, and a gun already pointed at him. His dad disappeared for a month.
- In the army, he's picked to be the translator for a US colonel. Three weeks in the jungle, shot at, and he knocks down a wall at Pablo Escobar's jail with a grenade launcher.
- A new kid in Mississauga who can't speak English trains a hardflip in his basement for months, lands it once at the mall, and becomes "Ali."
- Lost everything, crying from 10 PM to 4 AM, he tells God "don't count with me" until he gets a clear message. The phone rings. "You are a creator."
- Mois raised about four million dollars and paid more than four million to creators.

**What does NOT belong here.**
- A lesson with no scene. If there's no place, no person and no moment, it goes somewhere else or nowhere.
- Violence or trauma for shock with no turn.
- Any story that isn't in the book or the story library.

## Pillar 2: Founder of Your Own Life

**Description.** Alejo teaching the creative, unusual ways he rebuilds himself and his life, always grounded in something he actually did. His AuDHD brain as the operating system. Naming his devil Milton. Starting from zero again. Building a life around what he loves and cutting what he hates. Using AI so one mind can do what used to take hundreds of people. Each piece gives the viewer one thing to try, told through his story.

**Audience.** The person who's been trying and struggling and feels the system wasn't made for them. They've seen a few of his stories and now want to know how. Warm, middle of funnel.

**Business outcome.** Community members. This pillar is a preview of what the community gets.

**Example topics.**
- He named the darkness in his head Milton, after *Outwitting the Devil*. When it shows up at 3 AM he says "there's Milton again." How naming your devil takes away its power.
- His hate list (fundraising, admin, legal) and his love list (creating, building, marketing). He rebuilt his work around the second one.
- Starting over for the fourth time in Vancouver, where his whole history meant nothing. "Nothing has never stopped me. It's just the starting position."
- MidJourney felt like the starting gun: one creative mind can now do what took millions of dollars and hundreds of people.
- Why he can't be touched lightly or walk barefoot on some surfaces, and how knowing he's AuDHD changed the way he builds his days.

**What does NOT belong here.**
- Generic productivity or morning-routine tips.
- Money listicles, get-rich promises, "100 ways to..." content.
- Named frameworks with no story behind them ("The Energy Audit").
- AI tool tutorials that could come from anyone.

---

## Pillar 3: Force of Nature

**Description.** Alejo in motion right now, Casey Neistat style. Behind the scenes of whatever he's building, shooting, writing or speaking at this week. The book in progress. Talks and stages. Videos he makes alone with AI. Live proof that the guy in the stories is still doing it.

**Audience.** People deciding whether he's the real thing: event organizers, podcast hosts, and followers close to joining the community. They need to see him act, now.

**Business outcome.** Speaking gigs, plus trust that turns followers into community members.

**Example topics.**
- The run-up to the October 1 Digital Marketing Summit talk: building it, rehearsing it, backstage, and what happened on stage.
- One day, one creator, one film: making a full piece alone with AI, start to finish.
- Building the Wonder Years book: finding the manuscript, the editorial system, the scenes that almost didn't make it.
- "Where my time went": a real week, shown on screen.

**What does NOT belong here.**
- Polished announcements ("excited to share," "humbled to announce").
- Client work showcases.
- Hype about what he's going to do with nothing made yet.

---

## Which pillar to feed most

The Wonder Years. It's the one thing nobody else can copy, it builds the audience the other two need, and it feeds book readers and keynotes directly. A rough weekly split: 50% Wonder Years, 30% Founder of Your Own Life, 20% Force of Nature. Around a talk or a book launch, Force of Nature goes up.
