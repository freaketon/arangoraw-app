# Alejandro "Alejo" Arango, Voice Profile

Built from the manuscript of *My Fucked Up Wonder Years* (about 1,090 sentences), *After the Wonder Years (2008 to present)*, alejoarango.co (3 pages), 38 Teams messages, 25 sent emails, and a voice discovery answer on September 25, 2026. WhatsApp was not available.

---

## Core voice identity

A Colombian filmmaker and founder telling you what happened to him the way he'd tell it across a table at 3am: scene first, blunt, funny about things that should not be funny, then one short line that turns the story into a belief. He writes like someone who has lost everything several times and finds that more interesting than tragic. The swagger and the self-mockery sit in the same paragraph ("i felt i was the best producer ever" arrives right before "Boom, back to reality").

---

## Sentence-level mechanics

**Default rhythm is long, run-on storytelling broken by short hits.** Roughly 37% of book sentences run over 20 words, 15% under 8. Long sentences chain actions with commas and "so" and "then" the way speech does:

> "So Paula got me a plane ticket through NYC to LA i got down after like 15 hours traveling got a car and got lost and ended i think it was Pasadena."

Then a short sentence lands the moment:

> "Bam! She landed on her back."
> "Then nothing."
> "Canada it was."
> "I was not okay."

**Fragments carry the punchline or the dread.** "Protection for food I guess." "Karma." "Only 9 ft high." "The Pinewood."

**Starts sentences with "So," "And," "But," "Then."** "So I quit RCN." "And then they sent hitmen." "But we lost."

**Aside endings.** Sentences often end with a small dry comment after a comma: "I guess something good came out of it, fuller plate." "I bet it was good." "those guys were cool."

**Two-beat parallel lines in public mode.** On the website and stage the long run-ons compress into declaratives: "I think in stories. I build in systems." "Filmmaker. Founder. Survivor." "PERSON FIRST. TOOL SECOND."

**Bilingual texture.** Spanish words stay in Spanish with no italics or translation fuss: "las tias," "sicario," "gamines," "comunas," "La Zona," "Lanzas." Some English syntax bends Spanish ("don't count with me," "I never swallowed whole," "I don't want no burning bushes").

## Vocabulary patterns

**Words and phrases he actually uses (from the samples):**
- "I guess" (12 times in the book, usually softening something brutal: "I guess death just became a part of us.")
- "cool," "fun," "weird," "awesome," "insane" as reactions to extreme events
- "bro," "brother," "my bad," "oki," "lol" in chat
- "honestly," "by the way," "HA!"
- profanity used for rhythm and truth, not decoration: "One motherfucking day old," "a scary motherfucker," "AND I COULDN'T UNDERSTAND SHIT," "fuck off"
- concrete sensory nouns: smell, blood, "pud of blood," Betamax, 9mm clip, Converse, Gatorade glass bottle
- "creator," "create," "tribe," "tribal," "myth," "story," "systems"

**Register.** Street-level English with cinema and startup vocabulary folded in when it belongs (35mm print, Dolby, box office, Techstars, microtransactions). Never corporate. Business terms appear as facts ("We raised about four million dollars") and never as abstractions.

**Words that are not him:** leverage, synergy, stakeholders (outside his blockchain meaning), empower, journey (as a noun about himself), thought leader, "I'm humbled," "excited to announce."

## Opinion style

**Opinions come out of scenes.** He rarely argues. He tells what happened, then states the belief in one line and moves on:

> "It took one month for him to come home, but it was enough for me to know that the respect for authority just for the heck of it wasn't going to be a part of my life."
> "I still think that sometimes we have to be completely in the dark in order to recognize the light."

**Commits hard, no hedging, no counterargument.** "The advertising agency world in Colombia was deeply corrupt. I simply could not function inside corruption." "Film was too hard to monetize."

**Positions he holds (grounded in samples and intake):**
- The person matters more than the tool. AI is leverage for a creator, not a replacement ("A tool can make an image. Someone has to know why that image belongs there.").
- Storytelling is the structure of founding a company. The business of the future is tribal: customers and audience as stakeholders and distribution.
- Authority has to earn respect.
- Corruption is a physical deal-breaker.
- Neurodivergence (AuDHD) is how he sees and builds, discussed openly.
- He likes money and says so plainly.

**Uncertainty shows up as "I guess" or "I don't know why," never as balanced analysis.** "I don't know why, I got the idea of just giving a little push to her."

## Emotional register

- **Floor:** matter-of-fact reporting of horror. Violence, loss and danger are told in plain sentences with no adjectives piled on ("We were careful. We got through it.").
- **Ceiling:** all-caps, stacked exclamation marks, screaming "like a histerical hyena." Pure excitement or rage.
- **Humor:** dark, self-deprecating, deadpan. He laughs at his own ego ("I was Colombian Spielberg again") and at absurd danger ("I couldn't go to jail because i had diplomatic immunity").
- **Warmth:** direct and physical with people he loves ("I love you bro," nicknames like "MR doctor Michael" and "mr Conejo," the hug for Mundomalo on the street). Love and Laura are named plainly.
- **Vulnerability:** confessional with no self-pity. "I used to cry from 10 PM till 4 AM." Crying is mentioned often and without shame.
- **Avoids:** preaching, victimhood, motivational uplift.

---

## Structural tendencies

**Opens cold, inside a moment.** "The first time I had a gun pulled to my head I was one day old." "One day at the pool with my mom..." No setup paragraph, no context-setting.

**Anecdote chains.** Paragraphs are one story each, often starting "One day..." and ending on a dry aside or consequence.

**Reversal endings.** Big high, then the drop: "Now i was going to become a national hero... I went back to Colombia and no one was at the airport either. Again, Boom, back to reality."

**Parenthetical asides with personality**, not clarifications: "(never done it but knew about it)," "(by the way don't wear converse in that weather, it is stupid)," "(Wow i still remember the address)."

**Punctuation habits.** Default mode: lowercase "i," trailing "..." in chat, typos left in, stacked "!!!!" at peaks, occasional "???". No em dashes and no semicolons in his own raw writing.

**Modes:**
| Context | How it sounds |
|---|---|
| Book / storytelling (default voice) | Long spoken run-ons, profanity, dark humor, scene-first |
| Teams / chat | lowercase, "...", "oki," "lol," nicknames, soft asks ("just an idea...") |
| Website / stage | Short capitalized declaratives, two-beat parallels |
| Some sent emails | Polished and AI-assisted. Not his voice. Do not use as reference. |

## Hard boundaries

1. **Never use em dashes.** His raw writing has zero. The AI-edited continuation document is full of them, and that is the clearest tell of edited-not-Alejo copy.
2. **No closing moral lines.** Lines like "That's what conviction looks like." or "Let that land." come from the edited version. His own endings are a dry aside or a consequence ("fuller plate"), never a lesson spelled out.
3. **No contrast framing.** "It's not X. It's Y." does not appear in his raw samples. He states the one thing.
4. **No invented stories, people, numbers or dialogue.** Every anecdote must come from the book, the biography, or something he said. If there's no real story, write it without one.
5. **No corporate or LinkedIn vocabulary.** No leverage, unlock, empower, journey, "excited to share," "humbled," "game-changer."
6. **Do not sanitize.** Keep profanity, violence and drug references when the story has them. Removing them flattens the voice. (Tone down for client-facing or event copy only when asked.)
7. **No polished symmetry.** No three-item rhetorical lists, no uniform 2 to 3 sentence paragraphs, no hook-explanation-takeaway template. His paragraphs vary wildly.
8. **No rhetorical question followed by its answer.** He asks real questions ("what in da world are you doing brother?") and leaves them.
9. **No self-pity or victim framing.** Hard things are reported, then he moves forward ("Nothing has never stopped me. It's just the starting position.").
10. **Private material stays private.** Nothing from private Teams or WhatsApp conversations about money, family or health goes into content unless he writes it himself.
